**** Job Description and Duties ****
Interested in digging into why people do what they do? Like to work
independently but with a supportive team to bounce ideas off of and learn from?
Want to actually see the value you add to a business? The Business Planning &
Research Unit at the California Lottery is dedicated to maximizing sales and
contributions to California public schools through thoughtful evaluations of
marketing initiatives and enterprise programs, and conducting consumer research
and analytics to drive key business and marketing strategy decisions. And we
need another creative, analytical thinker to join our team!

This position requires a current Research Data Analyst I exam. If you need to
take the exam or if your score has expired, click hereto gain list eligibility.

This position is headquartered in Sacramento and is eligible for a hybrid
telework schedule. Employees must be able to report to their assigned
headquarters office as assigned. Travel expense to and from the assigned
headquarters are the responsibility of the employee. Eligibility for telework
requires the employee to reside in California per California Government Code
Section 14200 which states: âtelecommuting means the partial or total
substitution of computers or telecommunication technologies, or both, for the
commute to work by employees residing in California.

When choosing a state organization to work for, there are a lot of options. Why
choose the California State Lottery?  

People are drawn to the Lottery because we:
    * Are an energetic, creative, and talented team
    * Value diversity and inclusion in the workplace 
    * Empower team members to bring their knowledge, expertise, and innovation
    * Recognize individual and team contributions
    * Support the importance of work-life balance

    * Are dedicated to preserving our environment by lowering our carbon
      footprint

**** Desirable Qualifications ****
In addition to evaluating each candidate's relative ability, as demonstrated by
quality and breadth of experience, the following factors will provide the basis
for competitively evaluating each candidate:
    * Degree in statistics, economics, data analysis, quantitative research, or
      psychology.
    * Knowledge and experience conducting quantitative analysis on programs or
      initiatives.
    * Knowledge and experience with the design and execution of both
      qualitative and quantitative research methodologies.
    * Knowledge and/or experience conducting data manipulation, aggregations,
      and analysis of structured and unstructured data using Excel.
    * Experience using SPSS (or other statistical software), Power BI (or other
      visualization tool), R (or other predictive modeling software), and/or
      survey data analysis software.
    * Knowledge of utilizing the scientific method for research.
    * Knowledge of time management and organizational techniques to provide for
      efficient prioritization and completion of competing work assignments.
    * Experience writing technical reports and presenting data and statistical
      analysis to others.
    * Strong written and verbal communication skills.
    * Ability to convey and explain complex problems and solutions to a
      technical and non-technical audience.
