**** Job Description and Duties ****
The Enforcement Division's Portable Equipment Operations Section (PEOS) is
seeking a productive, detail-oriented individual to become a member of its
Portable Equipment Registration Program (PERP) team to work closely with a
dynamic group of Air Pollution Specialists (APS), Air Resources Engineers
(ARE), Air Resources Technicians (ART), Office Technicians, Analysts, and a
Staff APS, and perform technical and administrative duties throughout the
portable equipment registration process. Responsibilities include working with
APSs and AREs in evaluating and processing portable registration applications,
determining eligibility of engines and equipment units, performing emissions
calculations for engineering evaluations, and computing emission limitations
for operating conditions. Data entry duties include entering application
requests and updating existing equipment and company data. It is pertinent for
ARTs to stay current with the PERP Regulation and associated Portable Engine
Airborne Toxic Control Measure (ATCM) in order to assist with technical
evaluations for portable engines and equipment units. Administrative duties
entail working with PERP analysts, APSs, and management to account for
overpayments and refunds, and assisting with answering questions and responding
to information requests from local air districts, governmental agencies, the
public, and regulated industry. Duties also include generating and preparing
portable registration documents, stickers, and placards for mailing; scanning
and/or indexing PERP applications and related documents; assisting with
organizing a weekly schedule for all PERP tasks within this classification; and
performing other administrative support tasks within the Section and/or Branch
as needed.
You will find additional information about the job in the Duty_Statement.

**** Desirable Qualifications ****
In addition to evaluating each candidate's relative ability, as demonstrated by
quality and breadth of experience, the following factors will provide the basis
for competitively evaluating each candidate:
Successful candidates will be attentive to detail and have strong math,
science, and organizational skills. Also, basic knowledge of air pollution
control, internal combustion engine operation, and California's air pollution
control regulatory process is desirable. Ideal candidates will be flexible,
able to work effectively in a team setting and individually, have excellent
interpersonal skills, meet deadlines, follow written and verbal instructions,
use tact and good judgment when working with people, apply good customer
service techniques, and work effectively under pressure with a high volume
workload. Applicants should be familiar with Microsoft/Office 365 (including
Outlook, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint), Microsoft Teams, Adobe Acrobat, and
Windows. The applications will be screened and only the most qualified
candidates will be interviewed.
