**** Department Information ****
The mission of California State Parks is to provide for the health,
inspiration, and education of the people of California by helping to preserve
the state's extraordinary biological diversity, protecting its most valued
natural and cultural resources, and creating opportunities for high-quality
outdoor recreation.
All employees are responsible for contributing to an inclusive, safe, and
secure work environment that values diverse cultures, perspectives, and
experiences, and is free from discrimination.
